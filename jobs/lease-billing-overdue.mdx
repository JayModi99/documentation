# Lease billing overdue job

`LeaseBillingOverdueBackgroundService`  
Config: `Lease:BillingOverdue:Enabled`, `IntervalMinutes` (default **60**).

Also: `LeaseStatusReconciliationService` runs **once at startup** to close fully-returned leases.
