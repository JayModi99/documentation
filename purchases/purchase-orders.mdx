# Purchase orders

## Status machine

```
Draft/Rejected ──submit──► PendingApproval OR Approved*
                              │
                         approve / reject
                              │
                           Approved
                      ╱               ╲
                 receive              amend (zero receipts)
                      ╲               ╱
                 PartiallyReceived
                      │
                 FullyReceived ──close──► Closed
```

\*Submit may skip to Approved when company approval settings allow.

## Key endpoints

Submit, approve, reject, receive, amend, payment, close, attachments, receipts, assets, activity, export, settings — see [API: Purchases](/api-reference/purchases).
