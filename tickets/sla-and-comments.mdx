# SLA & comments

- First **public agent** comment sets `FirstRespondedAt`
- `POST /api/v1/Ticket/{id}/comments` · Permission: `tickets.comment`
- Attachment upload-urls: `POST /api/v1/Ticket/attachment-upload-urls`
- Reopen clears resolution/closed timestamps, keeps assignee, resets SLA clocks
