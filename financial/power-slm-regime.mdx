# Power SLM regime

Company tax settings (`GET/PUT /api/v1/Depreciation/tax-settings`):

| Regime | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `WdvBlock` | Default Appendix I WDV blocks |
| `PowerSlm` | Appendix IA SLM on actual cost (Rule 5(1A)) |
| `PowerWdvBlock` | Power undertaking opted into WDV blocks (irreversible) |

`PowerUndertakingType`: Generation | GenerationAndDistribution.

Masters: `GET /api/v1/DepreciationMaster/income-tax-power`.
