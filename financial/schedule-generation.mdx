# Schedule generation

`POST /api/v1/Depreciation/{assetId}/generate` · Permission: `depreciation.generate`

## Gate

Requires `IsDepreciationEnabled` and `PutToUseDate`.

## Rules enforced

- Salvage &lt; cost
- Opening accum. dep. ≤ cost − salvage
- 180-day first-year half-rate for IA (day count)
- Locked periods preserved
- Dispose regenerates schedule ending on disposal date

View: `GET …/schedule?book=&view=&fy=&from=&to=`
