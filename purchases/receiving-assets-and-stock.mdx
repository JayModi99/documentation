# Receiving assets & stock

`POST /api/v1/PurchaseOrder/{id}/receive` with `ReceivePurchaseOrderRequest`.

## Guards

- Status Approved or PartiallyReceived
- Delivery site required
- Consumable lines need location
- Qty ≤ remaining

## Effects

- Creates `PurchaseOrderReceipt` (+ lines)
- Asset lines → new `Asset` rows at `Available` (linked via `PurchaseOrderReceiptAsset`)
- Consumable lines → receive stock transactions / balances
- GST treatment `Capitalize` vs `InputCredit` affects capitalized cost
