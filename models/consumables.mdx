# Consumable entities

| Entity | Role |
|--------|------|
| `ConsumableCategory` | Category master |
| `ConsumableUnitOfMeasure` | UoM master |
| `ConsumableModel` | Stockable SKU |
| `ConsumableModelPhoto` | Photos (primary flag) |
| `ConsumableStockBalance` | On-hand qty at (model, site, location) |
| `ConsumableTransaction` | Ledger of receive/issue/transfer/adjust/dispose/reversal |

Transaction types and stock effects are documented under [Consumable transactions](/consumables/transactions).
