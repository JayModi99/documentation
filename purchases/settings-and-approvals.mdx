# Purchase settings & approvals

| Method | Path | Permission |
|--------|------|------------|
| GET | `/api/v1/PurchaseOrder/settings` | `purchase.view` |
| PUT | `/api/v1/PurchaseOrder/settings` | `purchase.edit` |

Settings drive whether submit lands in `PendingApproval` or auto-`Approved` (`RequirePoApproval` / amount thresholds on the company).
