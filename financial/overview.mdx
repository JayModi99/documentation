# Financial operations overview

AssetMate Pro tracks cost at the asset register and runs a dual-book depreciation engine (Income Tax **IA** and Companies Act **CA**). Related money flows:

- Lease billing records + overdue job
- Maintenance cost lines
- Purchase order payment fields and close rules
- Dispose proceeds / values

Primary API: `/api/v1/Depreciation`.
