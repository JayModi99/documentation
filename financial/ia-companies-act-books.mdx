# IA & Companies Act books

| Book | Typical method |
|------|----------------|
| IA | Block WDV (statutory rate); or Power SLM if company regime is `PowerSlm` |
| CA | Company/site method — SLM or WDV |

Schedules are independent per asset per book. Snapshot APIs:

- `GET /api/v1/Depreciation/{assetId}/snapshot?book=&asOfDate=`
- `GET /api/v1/Depreciation/{assetId}/dual-snapshot`
