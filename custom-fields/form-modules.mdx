# Form modules

Frontend/backend catalog includes: Assets, Dispose, Maintenance, Checkout, Checkin, Reserve, Lease, LeaseReturn, User, Sites, Locations, Departments, Categories, Brands, Models, Partners, AssetKits, Tickets, PurchaseOrders.

Category scope `Limited` is allowed **only** on the Assets form.
